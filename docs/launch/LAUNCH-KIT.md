## Product Hunt draft — use after the pilot

Name: NekuLoader
Tagline: Save authorized audio. Play your local library.

Description:
NekuLoader combines audio downloading, artwork, a searchable local library and
playback in a pixel-styled desktop app. Free proprietary alpha. Mac Apple silicon
is signed and notarized; Windows and Debian packages are experimental.

Maker comment:
I'm sharing NekuLoader's public alpha and would love feedback from people who
keep audio files locally. The workflow to test is simple: install the app,
install the required components, save a recording you have permission to
download, then find and play it in the library.

The visual direction is black and pixel-styled. Catalog links from Spotify,
Deezer and Apple Music use audio matching; the match may differ from the source
catalog. Native Windows feedback is still needed, so that package is labeled as
a testing build.

What's the most frustrating part of managing your local audio library today?

Required assets still to supply: final demonstration video and selected gallery
images. Use the real app icon and actual screenshots; make no claim of launch
ranking, users, ratings or testimonials.

## Real 30-second demonstration storyboard

- 0–3 seconds: show the black app interface; caption "Your audio. Your library."
- 3–7: briefly show Settings → Components on first run, or explicitly caption
  "Required components already installed" if recording a prepared machine.
- 7–15: paste an owned/licensed recording link, choose output and start. Label
  any edited wait "Download time shortened"; do not fabricate progress.
- 15–22: show the completed item in Library with artwork.
- 22–27: play, pause and seek; use audio cleared for promotional reuse or mute.
- 27–30: show https://nekudev.com/nekuloader/ and "Free public alpha".

Phone transfer is excluded until tested on physical devices. A screen recording
has not yet been produced. The licensed Blender test material used during QA
requires attribution if reused; use your own recording for the simplest demo.
